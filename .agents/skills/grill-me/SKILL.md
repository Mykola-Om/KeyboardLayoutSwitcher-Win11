# Grill Me

Run a relentless interview to sharpen the user's plan or design. 

## Instructions

1. Interview the user relentlessly about every aspect of their proposed plan or design until you reach a shared understanding.
2. Walk down each branch of the decision tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one.
3. For each question, provide your recommended answer.
4. Ask the questions **one at a time**, waiting for feedback on each. Do NOT present a list of multiple questions at once.
5. If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead of asking the user.
6. Do not start implementing the plan/code until the user explicitly approves and confirms a shared understanding has been reached.
